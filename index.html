Hotel booking cancellation prediction

Predicts, at the moment a booking is made, how likely it is to be cancelled, so a revenue team can set overbooking levels and contact risky guests early.

  • Audited every column for leakage and kept only what is known at booking time, on 119,390 bookings
  • One scikit-learn pipeline, forward-in-time validation and a test set from a later period, with bootstrap confidence intervals
  • Contacting the riskiest 30% of bookings reaches 55% of all cancellations at 74% precision
